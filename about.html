About Lami Nnamonu

Professor Lami Nnamonu PMP FICCON, FCSN, FSTAN, CFPM Department of Environmental Sustainability, Joseph Sarwaun Tarka University Makurdi 971010, Benue State.

After graduating in Division One of the West African School Certificate Examination from the Federal Government College Kano, Lami proceeded to the University of Jos and bagged a First-Class BSc degree in Chemistry Education. In her first year of university, she won the Federal Government National Merit Award and was on that Scholarship throughout her undergraduate studies. Best graduating student MSc Organic Chemistry at the University of Nigerian Nsukka with a CGPA of 3.91 out of 4.00, Lami Nnamonu did her PhD course work in University of Agriculture Makurdi and won the Commonwealth Split-site Doctoral Scholarship which took her to carry out PhD research in Durham University, England United Kingdom. This led to a PhD in Agrochemical Technology – the first PhD in that discipline in sub-Saharan Africa. She earned a perfect CGPA of 5.0 (on a scale of 5.0) in the PhD, a performance that earned her automatic employment as Lecturer 1 in the University of Agriculture Makurdi. From this appointment as the first female staff in chemistry department, she rose to become the first female Professor of Chemistry in Benue State.

While carrying out her PhD research at Durham University UK, Lami’s academic poster won international acclaim as one of the Best three Poster Prizes at the Symposium of Physical Organic Chemists (SYMPOC) held in Strathclyde University, Glasgow Scotland, United Kingdom. Her PhD research earned national distinction as “Best PhD Thesis in Physical Sciences During 2011,” an award was by the National Universities Commission (NUC) under the Nigerian Universities Doctoral Theses Award Scheme (NUDTAS). Her thesis was adjudged the best in Physical Sciences – Chemistry, Mathematics, Physics, Computer Science and Statistics in all of Nigeria.

Distinguished scholar and accomplished scientist, Lami Nnamonu is a two-time winner of the highly coveted Schlumberger Foundation Faculty for the Future Post-Doctoral Fellowship Award during which she was Research Fellow at two Universities in England – Newcastle University and Durham University – for two years.

She was Head of Department of Pre-Science Department and Acting Director of the Centre for Agrochemical Technology in the Federal University of Agriculture Makurdi (now Joseph Sarwaun Tarka University Makurdi.) Lami is currently the maiden Project Manager (Centre Leader) of the Centre for Innovation in Procurement Environmental and Social Standards (CIPESS), a World Bank supported Sustainable Procurement Environmental and Social Standards Enhancement (SPESSE) Project in the University. The project she leads caters for training in all six states of the North Central geo-political zone of Nigeria and the Federal Capital Territory. She is the first Director of the Institute of Procurement Environmental and Social Standards (IPESS) at JOSTUM, the first of such Institutes in Nigeria.

A World Bank trained Environmental Standards facilitator, Lami Nnamonu is a certified Project Management Professional (PMP) of the Project Management Institute (PMI) Pennsylvania USA, having passed the dreaded examination Above Target (AT) at the first attempt. Certified in Alternative Dispute Resolution, Lami has physically participated twice in the global Project Control Summit organized by the Project Control Academy in 2024 and 2025 at Galveston, Texas USA, winning for two years in a row, the Technology Explorer Award 2024 & 2025. She won the Summit Social Media Star Award in 2024 and the Best Video Communicator Award in May 2025 Project Control Summit at Galveston, USA. In June 2025, Lami earned a Certificate of Achievement in the Dale Carnegie Course® at Calgary Canada, and won the globally sought-after Dale Carnegie Pen for Outstanding Performance in Communication.

An international endorser of the Haggai Institute International, Lami won the Communication Award at the Haggai Institute Training in Singapore (Session 554-555) for World Leaders in 2005. She was the Diocesan Communicator for Otukpo Diocese of The Church of Nigeria Anglican Communion Benue State, for eight years. An entrepreneurial scientist & Associate Member of the Chartered Institute of Business Entrepreneurs USA, Lami is a Faculty Advisor of JOSTUM team of Enactus since 2008 from where she became the first and only Nigerian representative at the Global Faculty Research Network (GFRN) of Enactus, the largest international student organization in the world – http//www.enactus.org – which uses the power of entrepreneurial action to enable progress around the world.

Professor Lami Nnamonu is Faculty Advisor of the American Chemical Society International Students’ Chapter which she established and has been nurturing in the University since 2015. The National Project Coordinator of the RSquaredP (Recruitment, Retention & Progression) Workgroup, an initiative of the Schlumberger Foundation Faculty for the Future Fellows and Alumnae, working to bridge the gender gap in Science Technology Engineering and Mathematics (STEM), from where she became National Secretary of the Network of Nigerian Women in STEM (NWISTEM). Lami is founder and Chief Executive Officer of her pet project, Network for Young Girls in Future Technology and Science (GIFTS) Initiative, mentoring girls into tertiary and postgraduate STEM studies and careers.

The First Benue State Best Teacher of the Year, Lami Nnamonu has a number of prizes and awards including University of Jos Prize for Best Student in Faculty; Prof P. N. Lassa Prize for Best Final Year Student and Students’ National Merit Award throughout her undergraduate years. The Environmental Champion 2025,

A bona fide member of the International Union of Pure and Applied Chemistry (IUPAC), she’s a certified organizer of the annual IUPAC Global Women Breakfast and an invited speaker at the IUPAC/Canadian CCE conference 2020 and several other international meetings.

A widely travelled invited speaker at several conferences and workshops, Lami is active in many learned societies - she’s Fellow of the Institute of Chartered Chemists of Nigeria (FICCON), Fellow of the Chemical Society of Nigeria (FCSN), Fellow of the Science Teachers’ Association of Nigeria (FSTAN), and Chartered Fellow of Project Managers of Nigeria (CFPM). Member of the Organization of Women in Science for the Developing World (OWSD); American Chemical Society (ACS); Royal Society of Chemistry (RSC); Nigerian Environmental Society (NES); Project Management Institute (PMI) USA & Nigeria; the global Project Professionals Network (PPN) and the Teachers’ Registration Council of Nigeria (TRCN). Lami has participated in several international trainings, conferences and workshops across Africa and the rest of the world, and published many peer-reviewed academic articles in national and international journals.

Lami is on the Editorial Board of many academic journals and a reviewer of scientific articles for many publishing houses. She speaks English, a little French, Hausa, Idoma, Igala, Igbo, and Yoruba. She enjoys dance aerobics, cycling and singing.
